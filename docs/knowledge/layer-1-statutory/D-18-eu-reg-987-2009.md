## The three provisions a dossier needs

**What "substantial part" means** — Article 14(8), quoted verbatim:

> For the purposes of the application of Article 13(1) and (2) of the basic
> Regulation, a 'substantial part of employed or self-employed activity' pursued
> in a Member State shall mean a quantitatively substantial part of all the
> activities … **without this necessarily being the major part** of those
> activities. To determine whether a substantial part of the activities is
> pursued in a Member State, the following indicative criteria shall be taken
> into account: (a) in the case of an employed activity, **the working time
> and/or the remuneration**; … In the framework of an overall assessment, **a
> share of less than 25 % in respect of the criteria mentioned above shall be an
> indicator that a substantial part of the activities is not being pursued** in
> the relevant Member State.

**Who decides, and in what order** — Article 16(1)–(3), abridged and quoted:

> 1. A person who pursues activities in two or more Member States shall **inform
> the institution designated by the competent authority of the Member State of
> residence** thereof.
> 2. The designated institution of the place of residence shall **without delay
> determine the legislation applicable** … That initial determination shall be
> **provisional**. …
> 3. The provisional determination … shall become **definitive within two
> months** of the institutions designated by the competent authorities of the
> Member States concerned being informed of it …

**What the "A1" legally is** — Article 19(2), quoted in full:

> At the request of the person concerned or of the employer, the competent
> institution of the Member State whose legislation is applicable pursuant to
> Title II of the basic Regulation shall provide **an attestation that such
> legislation is applicable** and shall indicate, where appropriate, **until
> what date and under what conditions**.

**Note that neither regulation ever uses the name "A1."** "A1" is the portable
document designation given by the Administrative Commission; the legal object is
the Article 19(2) attestation. A dossier that says "A1 required" is naming a form
rather than the instrument, which is fine for a specialist and wrong for a
citation.

## What this settles

`a1_certificate_recommended` in `src/uc04/riskMatrix.js` is a label with, until
now, nothing behind it. This document supplies the three things it was missing:
**who issues** (the competent institution of the state whose legislation
applies), **on whose request** (the person or the employer), and **in what
order** (residence-state institution first, provisionally, definitive after two
months). The two-month clock is operationally the point: it is longer than most
workations.
