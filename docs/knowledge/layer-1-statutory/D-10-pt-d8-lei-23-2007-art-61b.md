## How it was retrieved, after a pass that could not

Every DRE **HTML** route is still an OutSystems application shell — HTTP 200,
~2.3 KB, `<noscript>JavaScript is required</noscript>`. Nothing about that has
changed. What changed is that **`files.diariodarepublica.pt` is now
allowlisted**, and it serves the *gazette itself* as a static PDF under a path
the HTML site never links in a form a crawler can follow:

```
https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/gratuitos/1s/<yyyy>/<mm>/<issue><suffix>.pdf
```

`16400` is issue **164**, suffix `00` (no supplement). The per-act path
(`/1s/2022/08/16400/0000200005.pdf`) and the ELI path (`/eli/lei/18/2022/…`)
both fail — `301` to an error page and `403` respectively. **Only the
`gratuitos` whole-issue form works.** That single URL shape is the finding that
unblocked D-10, D-02 and D-03 in one go.

The four DRE identifiers confirmed by the previous pass from the Ministry of
Foreign Affairs' own link list remain correct and are kept above; they are how
the issue number was found. `dre.pt` itself is now reachable and is purely a
redirector — every path 301s to `diariodarepublica.pt`, so it adds nothing.

## Article 61.º-B, quoted in full

> **Artigo 61.º-B**
> **Visto de residência para o exercício de atividade profissional prestada de
> forma remota para fora do território nacional**
>
> É concedido a trabalhadores subordinados e profissionais independentes visto
> de residência para o exercício de atividade profissional prestada, de forma
> remota, a pessoas singulares ou coletivas com domicílio ou sede fora do
> território nacional, devendo ser demonstrado o vínculo laboral ou a prestação
> de serviços, consoante o caso.

*Working translation, a reading aid and not a source: employees and independent
professionals are granted a residence visa for professional activity performed
remotely for natural or legal persons domiciled or seated outside national
territory; the employment relationship or the provision of services must be
demonstrated, as the case may be.*

**That is the entire article. Fifty-two words, one paragraph, no subparagraphs.**

## The second instrument, which the same law also creates — art. 54.º(1)(i)

The consular authority (D-11) described **two** remote-work routes and the
manifest treated that as an administrative distinction. It is statutory. Lei
18/2022 also amends the **temporary-stay visa**:

> **Artigo 54.º · Visto de estada temporária**
>
> **1** — O visto de estada temporária destina-se a permitir a entrada e a
> estada em território nacional **por período inferior a um ano** para: […]
> **i)** Exercício de atividade profissional subordinada ou independente,
> prestada, **de forma remota**, a pessoa singular ou coletiva com domicílio ou
> sede fora do território nacional; […]
>
> **4** — A emissão do visto de estada temporária previsto na alínea *i)* do
> n.º 1 carece de demonstração do vínculo laboral ou da prestação de serviços,
> consoante o caso.

So the statute itself sets **one** duration limit on **one** of the two routes:
the temporary-stay visa is for a period of **less than one year**. The residence
visa (art. 61.º-B) carries no duration of its own — it is the gateway to an AIMA
residence permit, which is D-12's subject and is still unretrieved.

## What the article does **not** say, which is the whole point

`DNV_COUNTRIES_PROVENANCE.needsForAVersionedTable` in `src/uc04/riskMatrix.js`
asks for *"the scheme's own thresholds — income floor, insurance, maximum
duration — so that membership becomes a condition the request is checked against
rather than a blanket exemption."*

**Article 61.º-B contains none of them.** No income figure, no insurance
requirement, no maximum duration, no reference to an index. There is nothing in
this article to encode, and a table row citing "Lei 23/2007 art. 61.º-B" for a
euro figure would be citing a provision that does not contain one.

The conditions exist — they sit in the **general** residence-visa conditions of
art. 52.º, in a *portaria* on means of subsistence that art. 52.º(1)(d) delegates
to, and in what the consular network requires in practice (**D-11**: *"four
monthly minimum guaranty remuneration"*, a multiple of the **RMMG**). Three
instruments, three publishers, one gate. That distribution is itself the finding,
and it is recorded as [`CONTRADICTIONS.md`](CONTRADICTIONS.md) **C-15** and
**C-17**.

## What is still open

1. **This is the enacting text, not the current consolidation.** The MNE's own
   link list names **13 subsequent amendments** to Lei 23/2007, the most recent
   there being Lei n.º 56/2023. Whether any of them touched art. 61.º-B or
   art. 54.º(1)(i) has **not** been established: each would require its own
   gazette issue, and DRE's consolidated view — the one place that answers the
   question in one read — remains unreadable here. The sidecar says so rather
   than presenting a 2022 text as a 2026 text.
2. **D-12 (AIMA) is still class D** — `aima.gov.pt` completes the proxy
   `CONNECT` and then serves an incomplete certificate chain. The residence
   *permit* half of the scheme therefore still has no authority behind it.
