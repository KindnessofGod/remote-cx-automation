## The three articles the A1 question turns on

> **Article 11 — General rules**
>
> 1. Persons to whom this Regulation applies shall be subject to the
> legislation of **a single Member State only**. …
>
> 3. Subject to Articles 12 to 16: (a) **a person pursuing an activity as an
> employed or self-employed person in a Member State shall be subject to the
> legislation of that Member State**; …

> **Article 12 — Special rules**
>
> 1. A person who pursues an activity as an employed person in a Member State
> on behalf of an employer which normally carries out its activities there and
> who is **posted** by that employer to another Member State to perform work on
> that employer's behalf shall continue to be subject to the legislation of the
> first Member State, **provided that the anticipated duration of such work does
> not exceed 24 months** and that he/she **is not sent to replace another posted
> person**.

> **Article 13 — Pursuit of activities in two or more Member States**
>
> 1. A person who normally pursues an activity as an employed person in two or
> more Member States shall be subject: (a) to the legislation of the Member
> State of residence **if he/she pursues a substantial part of his/her activity
> in that Member State**; or (b) if he/she does not … (i) to the legislation of
> the Member State in which the registered office or place of business of the
> undertaking or employer is situated …

## What this settles

`EU_EEA_FOR_A1` in `src/uc04/riskMatrix.js` now has a named instrument and a
consolidation date. **Article 11(3)(a) is the default the code does not state**:
absent a posting, the destination state's legislation applies from day one —
which is the opposite default from the one an unflagged trip implies.

The article that governs is **not** one flag. `docs/KNOWLEDGE-SOURCES.md` L1-04
already sorts this **SPLIT** — membership is a TABLE, *which article applies* is
CORPUS — and the retrieved text confirms why: Article 12 turns on an
*anticipated duration* and a *not-a-replacement* condition, Article 13 on a
*substantial part* assessment whose meaning lives in the implementing
regulation (D-18, Article 14(8)). Neither is a lookup.

Two numbers appear here that the repository does not have: **24 months**
(Art. 12(1)) and the **25 %** indicator (D-18 Art. 14(8)). They are recorded in
[`CONTRADICTIONS.md`](CONTRADICTIONS.md) **C-6** and **C-7** and are
deliberately **not** encoded in `src/`.
