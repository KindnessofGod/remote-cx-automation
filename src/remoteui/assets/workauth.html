Work authorization requests

Raised by an employee inside Remote's Request Hub and waiting on you, their employer. Approve or decline on the row.

⚠ Your decision here is the EMPLOYER's, and only the employer's. Remote's own Mobility Team reviews it separately afterwards. This system cannot perform that review and cannot observe its outcome, so nobody is cleared to travel by anything on this screen.
ℹ This is a stand-in, not Remote's real platform. Where this sits, and what the two verbs are

Remote publishes no endpoint that creates a work-authorization request — an employee raises one inside Remote's own Request Hub — and the Sandbox holds none. Requests badged stand-in in the queue are this repository's, so the employer's decision has something to be about. Every field on them is a property of Remote's own schema; the values are ours, and a decision on one is recorded here and never sent to Remote.

Where this sits

    What the two buttons send

    Approve sends approved_by_manager. Decline sends declined_by_manager, and Remote requires a reason with it — which is why declining on a row asks for one before it will send.
