# Third-party content

The MIT licence in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) covers this repository's own code and
documentation. It does **not** cover the material below, which is reproduced
from its publishers under their own terms.

This file exists separately from `LICENSE` so that automated licence detection
reads the MIT text unmodified. Splitting it changes nothing about the terms.

## Statutory and agency documents

`docs/knowledge/` mirrors statutory and agency documents from EU, US, Canadian
and Dutch authorities. Each file carries its own provenance header naming its
source, retrieval date, SHA-256 of the retrieved bytes, and licence basis —
CC0 1.0, Commission Decision 2011/833/EU, CC BY 4.0, Reproduction of Federal
Law Order SI/97-5, or US federal public domain.

Reproductions of Canadian federal law are not official versions.
